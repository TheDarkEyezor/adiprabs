# Just start building

As of late I've been seeing a lot of posts from founders who give the same starting advice to any aspiring builder: just start building. And yeah, I mean there's nothing I can say except for the fact that I completely agree with them.

It's all too easy to fall into the trap of trying to make something that's just better than what's already out there. More often than not you have well-meaning but somewhat direct people who would say "oh you know, nice project, but that's already been done. Why don't you work on something new?" The thing is, the world has everything, and it's hard to come up with something truly original unless you start building.

# Building leads to building

That's the thing - building more, or even coming up with an entirely new idea in a different domain, might just be the thing that helps skill you up in your domain of interest. It helps you start conversations with people you'd like to connect with, because builders who are curious are always willing to connect with people who share similar interests but are able to provide different perspectives. Starting your projects might be a segway into that as well.

And for stuff like robotics or hardware, I'd say yeah go for it as well. I'm sure you'll be able to find discount parts instead of the premium stuff, and it gives you the sort of reassurance that if it's working for cheaper models then it will definitely work for more premium parts.

# Taking the leap

After building my first product - now whether it's profitable or not remains to be seen - I'm glad I took that first step in creating something meaningful and just putting myself out there.
