# Welcome to My Blog! 👋

It took me a while to warm up to the idea of blogging. I thought it would be hectic to try and post a write up every week alongside college, founding a company, building cool apps, learning about quant, and even trying to have a social life.
But I've come around. I've realised that the blog gives me more of an online presence, and it presents a great opportunity for me to exercise the Feynman technique. It prompts me to reflect on what I've learned over the week and build something impressive.
And, I'd be taking a page out of all the articles that I've read thus far, helping me learn something new along the way, and it would be great if I could encourage one of you readers to be a builder yourself.

This is the first of many posts where I'll be sharing my thoughts, learnings, and projects. I will be attempting to sync this across various platforms so its accessible to anyone and everyone

## What to Expect

Here's just some topics that I'll be exploring

- **Tech Tutorials** - Deep dives into technologies I'm working with
- **AI & Machine Learning** - Explorations in the AI space
- **Quant and Statistics** - Some deep dives on quant techniques and building them (gonna have to learn a lot of LaTeX for this one)
- **Project Updates** - Behind-the-scenes of what I'm building
- **Personal Reflections** - My thoughts as a student who's trying to make sense of the world and make a name for himself

I'll also try to post more on Twitter since that's where most founders read, and I'd love to get the thoughts of engineers more experienced than myself. That's it for the hello world, see you down the line!
